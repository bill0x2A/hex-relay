      HOW TO PLAY

      Your triangle orbits the hexagon. Turn left or right to stay in the openings. A wall that reaches you from the front ends a solo run. The side of a wall only stops you.

      Battle is 1v1. Each player has an arena with the same walls and 3 hit points. Pass through a glowing pickup to get an item. Fire it to attack your rival's arena or to help your own. An attack shows a warning first. Fire an attack of your own during the warning to counter it. A lock stops your triangle: press left and right in turn to break it.

      The motion button stops the spin, the tilt, and the pulse.
